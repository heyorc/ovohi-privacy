Privacy Policy for OvoHi

OvoHi is a language learning and cross-cultural exchange platform for users around the world. It helps users reduce language, regional, and cultural barriers through multilingual communication, translation, content sharing, and culture-focused conversations.

At OvoHi, we treat privacy and security as important considerations when designing and operating our services. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information and make product decisions with respect for user privacy.

Before using OvoHi services, please carefully read this Privacy Policy. Unless you have read and accepted all terms of this policy, please stop using OvoHi services immediately. Once you check or tap “Agree to the Privacy Policy”, you are deemed to have fully understood and accepted this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the OvoHi app (“App”), OvoHi Web (“Web”), and other services operated under the OvoHi brand that reference this policy (collectively, the “Services”). Where the relevant features are available, the App and Web may share accounts and process information for accounts, profiles, community content, messaging, translation, customer support, payment verification, safety, and compliance. Features, interfaces, and processing may differ by client; this policy does not represent that every feature is available on every client.

2. Information We Collect

  • (1) Account Registration

When you register for OvoHi, you need to provide login information and certain basic details necessary to provide the service, such as an email address or phone number, username, gender, date of birth, country, language, and avatar. The username, email address, or phone number you provide may be used as your OvoHi login credential.

When completing your profile, you may share additional information, such as profile text, an avatar, and photos. To add photos, you may need to allow us to access your camera or photo library.

Only users who are at least 18 years old may register for or use OvoHi. Users under 18 may not register for or use the Services. We may process your date of birth or age confirmation to determine eligibility, prevent use by minors, protect safety, and meet compliance obligations. We may use currently available eligibility checks to refuse or stop a registration or use attempt identified as being made by a user under 18. Eligibility checks may not detect every case; if we later identify an account as held or used by a user under 18, we may restrict or terminate that account and handle related information in accordance with applicable law and the retention rules in this policy. There is no parental-consent exception to this age requirement.

We may process your language preferences to provide applicable interfaces and translation services.

  • (2) Sign in with Apple and Google

When you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we may process the login provider, provider account identifier, email address and its verification status, display-name or name fields, an ID token or access token, an authorization code, and security parameters such as a nonce, state value, or request identifier, as needed for the applicable login flow. We use this information to authenticate you, create or link an OvoHi account, prevent fraud, and protect login security. Apple and Google independently process information they receive under their respective policies. The fields actually provided depend on your chosen provider, authorization, and login configuration.

  • (3) User-Generated Content and Communications

When you use relevant features, we may process content that you provide, post, upload, or send, including profile text, usernames, avatars, images, videos, moments or feed content, comments, chat text, voice messages, and links, as well as report materials, appeal evidence, and customer-support communications.

We may process this information to route and deliver messages; display, store, or translate content; investigate reports; enforce our Terms of Service and community rules; handle appeals; prevent fraud and abuse; protect users and platform safety; preserve necessary evidence; and comply with applicable law.

  • (4) Device, Usage, and Operational Security Data

To protect your account, network, operational, and system security and to prevent fraud, abuse, phishing, malware, and technical failures, our servers may automatically record device identifiers, IP address, operating system, app version, language settings, crash logs, access time, feature usage, payment verification status, and similar technical information.

  • (5) Camera Function

After you enable camera permission, you may use this function to take photos or videos for upload and sharing. Even if you have enabled camera permission, we access the camera only when you actively use the relevant function in the app. We handle related content according to this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

  • (6) Storage Permission

We need access to your device storage/photo album permission when you upload or update your account avatar or publish images and videos. If you refuse to enable these permissions, we will not be able to provide the corresponding functions, but this will not affect your use of other platform features.

  • (7) Microphone Voice Function

When you send voice messages, microphone permission is required. Even if you have enabled microphone permission, we will only access the microphone when you actively tap the microphone icon in the app to capture voice information. If you do not use this function, you may deny this permission. This will not affect your use of other platform features.

  • (8) Web Cookies and Browser Storage

OvoHi Web may use first-party cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage to support login, security, and currently available features. These technologies may store access and refresh tokens for login and security verification; language and message-sound preferences; chat read or deletion state; transcription-task state; device state; badge or unread-count state; PayPal pending or completed order identifiers; and wallet preselection. Where applicable, session data is cleared when the relevant browser session or flow ends. Preferences and other persistent data may remain until overwritten, cleared by a currently supported logout-clearing flow, or cleared by you through your browser.

  • (9) Push Notifications

To prepare and provide push services, OvoHi uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM may process device and platform information, an FCM token, a local device identifier, app version, language, and the notification content or business data needed for delivery. This information is used to initialize and maintain push tokens, prepare or deliver service notices, security alerts, community messages, and membership notices, and troubleshoot delivery. Whether a notification is displayed on your device depends on device or operating-system notification permission and your notification settings.

  • (10) Translation

When you use translation, the text to be translated and target language may be sent to the translation provider enabled in the production environment. The provider may include Baidu Translation or MiniMax and may vary with available configuration. This processing is used to generate the translation you request.

  • (11) Registration Country Prefill

When registration country prefill is available, we may use icanhazip.com to obtain the device’s public IP address and provide that IP address to api.iplocation.net to infer a country suggestion and prefill the registration country. If you keep the prefilled country or select another country, that country may be processed when you submit registration as registration, profile, and account data described in item (1). A country suggestion inferred from an IP address does not represent a precise location.

  • (12) Payments and Transaction Verification
  • iOS App: In-app purchases or subscriptions on iOS are processed by the Apple App Store. We may receive and process product, order, or transaction identifiers; receipts or verification data; purchase or subscription status; and refund-related status provided by Apple to fulfill orders, verify entitlements, prevent fraud, keep accounting records, and provide customer support.
  • Android App: In-app purchases or subscriptions initiated through the OvoHi Android App are processed by Google Play. We may receive and process product, order, or purchase identifiers; purchase tokens or verification data; purchase or subscription status; and refund-related status provided by Google Play for the purposes described above.
  • Web: One-time payments initiated through OvoHi Web may be processed by PayPal. We may process PayPal order and transaction identifiers; payer identifiers or contact information PayPal makes available to us; product, amount, currency, and payment status; and refund, reversal, chargeback, dispute, and related risk records to complete and verify transactions, handle refunds or disputes, prevent fraud, keep accounting records, and provide customer support. PayPal independently determines how it processes certain personal information under its own policies and generally acts as an independent controller or an equivalent independent processing party under applicable data protection law. See the PayPal Privacy Statement.
  • (13) Third-Party Services and Cross-Border Processing

We may engage third-party service providers to process information on OvoHi’s behalf for hosting, security, customer support, push notifications, translation, infrastructure, and compliance support, and use applicable contractual and security measures to limit their processing purposes. Providers such as Apple, Google, and PayPal may also independently process information under their own policies for third-party login or payment data described in this policy.

OvoHi provides Services globally, so identity, push, translation, payment, and infrastructure providers may process personal information outside your country or region. Depending on applicable law and the specific processing arrangement, we use applicable contractual measures, access controls, encryption, or other lawful safeguards. This policy does not promise a particular storage country or region.
