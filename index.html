Privacy Policy for OvoHi

OvoHi is a language learning and cross-cultural exchange platform for users around the world. It helps users reduce language, regional, and cultural barriers through multilingual communication, translation, content sharing, and culture-focused conversations.

At OvoHi, we treat privacy and security as important considerations when designing and operating our services. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information and make product decisions with respect for user privacy.

Before using OvoHi services, please carefully read this Privacy Policy. Unless you have read and accepted all terms of this policy, please stop using OvoHi services immediately. Once you check or tap “Agree to the Privacy Policy”, you are deemed to have fully understood and accepted this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to websites, applications, events, and other services operated under the OvoHi brand. We refer to all of these as “Services” in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • (1) Account Registration

When you register for OvoHi, you need to provide login information and certain basic details necessary for us to provide the service, such as email address or phone number, username, gender, date of birth, country, language, and avatar. The username, email address, or phone number you provide will be used as your login credential for OvoHi.

When completing your profile, you may share additional information with us, such as personal introduction text and photos. To add photos, you may need to allow us to access your camera or album. This helps us provide better service and experience.

We collect your date of birth (year, month, and day) to determine whether you meet the age requirement. OvoHi is intended for users who are at least 18 years old, and users under 18 may not register for or use the service.

We collect your language to better provide translation services. Multilingual real-time translation is also one of our core features.

  • (2) User-Generated Content and Communications

When you use messages, voice features, profile content, moments, comments, images, videos, reports, customer support, or other community features, we may process the content you provide or send. This processing may be used to send, display, store, translate, deliver, secure, review reported content, provide customer support, and meet legal or compliance obligations.

  • (3) Device, Usage, and Operational Security Data

To protect your account, network, operational, and system security and to prevent fraud, abuse, phishing, malware, and technical failures, our servers may automatically record device identifiers, IP address, operating system, app version, language settings, crash logs, access time, feature usage, payment verification status, and similar technical information.

  • (4) Camera Function

After you enable camera permission, you may use this function to take photos or videos for upload and sharing. Even if you have enabled camera permission, we access the camera only when you actively use the relevant function in the app. We handle related content according to this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

  • (5) Storage Permission

We need access to your device storage/photo album permission when you upload or update your account avatar or publish images and videos. If you refuse to enable these permissions, we will not be able to provide the corresponding functions, but this will not affect your use of other platform features.

  • (6) Microphone Voice Function

When you send voice messages, microphone permission is required. Even if you have enabled microphone permission, we will only access the microphone when you actively tap the microphone icon in the app to capture voice information. If you do not use this function, you may deny this permission. This will not affect your use of other platform features.

  • (7) Push Notifications, Translation, Purchases, and Third-Party Services

If you enable push notifications, we may process notification tokens and related delivery data to send service notices, security reminders, community messages, and membership notices. Translation features may require processing text, voice, or other user-generated content through OvoHi systems or third-party service providers. In-app purchases, subscriptions, coins, and order verification may be processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, payment processors, and related service providers according to their rules. We may also use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, customer support, translation, payment processing, and compliance support, and we require them to process information only for authorized purposes.
